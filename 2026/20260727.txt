A philosophy borrowed without its commitments is not that philosophy. It's the brand.

Stoicism is attractive to management because it looks separable from its premises. You can teach equanimity, resilience, discipline, without accepting what the philosophy actually demands: that the good of others is a genuine end, not a means to output. A company that trains employees to withstand adversity in order to extract more performance has taken the vocabulary and reversed the logic. The employees get calmer. The company gets the surplus.

You can read [the tell](post.html?p=2026/20260723.txt) in what the practice is optimized for.

!!A practice divorced from what it refuses isn't a simplified version. It's something else.!!
