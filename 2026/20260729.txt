Michael Huemer, a philosopher, cites research showing that the heritability of political orientation is around 0.53 — not because beliefs are genetic, but because personality is, and personality shapes political alignment more than argument does. You don't reason your way to your tribe. You find the tribe that already fits who you are.

This accounts for a pattern that frustrates anyone who's tried to change minds: information crosses the aisle and comes out as confirmation. The framework processes the evidence and produces the familiar conclusion. Huemer's sharper observation is that ideologues on every side are reliably manipulated by stories that match their existing schema. Progressive and populist alike, both susceptible to the narrative that confirms.

That's not stupidity. It's what schemas do: they recognize pattern-matches faster than they evaluate arguments.

!!People don't hold ideologies because of ideas. They hold them because of who they are.!!
