Evolution doesn't filter for truth. The visual blind spot — where the optic nerve attaches to the retina — is a gap the brain fills with invented information, because seeing a consistent image is more adaptive than registering the hole. A working fiction beats a broken truth.

The same principle operates at civilizational scale. Every large society coordinates through shared narratives that aren't strictly accurate but are highly functional: they align behavior, generate trust, distribute roles. The founding myths, the religious frameworks, the ideological commitments — none survive scrutiny as literal descriptions of reality. That's beside the point. They weren't selected for accuracy.

Individual delusion is dangerous: ninety-nine people who believe the impossible fail, and one succeeds. But collective coordination may require fictions that no individual would endorse alone.

!!The line between civilization and shared delusion isn't where we prefer to draw it.!!
