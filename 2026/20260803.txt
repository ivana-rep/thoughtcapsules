2026_08_03 | The system with no object
↳ [back to full archive](2026/2026_full-archive.html)
↳ [back to index](index.html)

For most of human history, stress was accurate. Adrenaline sharpens the senses and readies the muscles. Cortisol slows digestion, immunity, reproduction, and redirects everything toward the threat in front of you. The chemistry evolved to answer real danger, and it answered well.

The brain hasn't updated since. It doesn't distinguish being hunted from losing a job: both trigger the same two-phase response, fast mobilization followed by a slower system built to sustain it.

!!Chronic stress is that response left running with nothing left to run from.!!

The damage isn't a malfunction. It's the same precise machinery, aimed at a threat that never resolves because it was never physical to begin with.
