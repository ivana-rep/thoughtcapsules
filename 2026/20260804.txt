2026_08_04 | Your threshold, not everyone's
↳ [back to full archive](2026/2026_full-archive.html)
↳ [back to index](index.html)

Clutter works like a seasonal allergy. Pollen affects everyone exposed to it, but only some people react, and only past a personal threshold. Below that line, nothing registers. Above it, the air turns dense and hard to breathe.

Disorder works the same way. What one person tolerates without noticing suffocates another completely, and the difference isn't willpower. It's sensitivity. You don't choose a low threshold for accumulation any more than you choose to be allergic to pollen.

The common mistake is treating the feeling as the problem: chasing relief from the suffocation instead of addressing the accumulation causing it.

!!Relief that only removes the feeling leaves the threshold exactly where it was.!!
