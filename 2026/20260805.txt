Is this stoic? gets asked constantly in philosophy communities, and it's usually the wrong question. Gregory Sadler proposes three better ones instead: why do you want to classify this in the first place. Is it worth your time and attention at all. And if it is, in what way is it compatible, or in tension, with how you're trying to live.

That first question matters beyond Stoicism. The urge to sort something into a category before asking why is the [same instinct](post.html?p=2026/20260212.txt) that reduces people to abstractions.

!!Stoicism was never meant to be a filing system. Turning it into one is the most reliable way to stop practicing it.!!
