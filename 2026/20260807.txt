Lao Tzu: mastering yourself is the real power. External power was always borrowed, and it always expires. Michael Ovitz, describing decades inside institutional power, made a related point from the other direction: influence rarely belongs to whoever holds the resources. It belongs to whoever shapes the story told about them.

Albert Ellis pushes the same idea further inward. Most misery isn't imposed. It's manufactured, out of beliefs you never examined about what you need and what you can't survive without. [Responsibility](post.html?p=2026/20260304.txt) for your reactions was never a consolation prize. It was where the actual power was.

!!The power that lasts was never the kind anyone could give you.!!
