Temperance gets flattened into self-control, as if resisting temptation were the whole of it. The Stoics meant something wider. Arius Didymus lists four components: self-mastery, yes, but also order, decorum, and modesty, each its own discipline.

Reducing a virtue to its most visible fragment is a familiar move, and [no virtue stands alone](post.html?p=2026/20260622.txt), not even inside itself.

!!What looks like one virtue is usually several, doing their work quietly enough to pass for one.!!
