↳ [back to index](index.html)

Hume treated likeability as a set of practices, not a trait you either have or lack: yielding space to other people's egos, carrying things lightly, genuine modesty, reading a room and adjusting your register, minding how much space you take up in someone else's presence.

None of it is performance. Evolution favored it. In small groups, whoever took without giving got left behind, and being left behind was often fatal.

[Choosing solitude well](post.html?p=2026/20260714.txt) makes for good company. These are the practices that make company good once you're actually in it.

!!Sociability was never a gift some people have. It's a set of practices anyone can run.!!
