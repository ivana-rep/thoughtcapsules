Seneca said no wind is favorable to someone who doesn't know which harbor they're sailing to. Direction has to exist before conditions can even be judged good or bad. Observation works the same way: noticing itself is a trainable skill, and two people watching the same event can end up living in different phenomenological worlds, built from years of practiced attention. [Purpose isn't handed to you](post.html?p=2026/20260217.txt) either. You form it by living, and once you have one, the same wind that felt hostile starts reading as useful.

!!Favorable was never a property of the wind. It's a relationship between the wind and where you've decided to go.!!
