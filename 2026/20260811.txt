In 1240, with Assisi under siege and Frederick II's mercenaries at the gates, [Clare of Assisi](https://ivana-rep.github.io/soul/post.html?p=saints/clare-of-assisi.txt) was too ill to stand. She had herself carried to the monastery wall anyway, holding the Blessed Sacrament instead of a weapon. The attackers withdrew. Her only action, in the moment that mattered most, was to stay exactly where she was.

[Presence isn't a technique you deploy in a crisis](post.html?p=2026/20260219.txt). It's what decades of already being there leaves behind.

!!Some acts of courage don't look like resistance. They look like refusing to move.!!
