Intentions live entirely in your head until an action translates them into something someone else can actually check. "I meant well" is a claim about the inside. What you did is the only evidence anyone else gets to examine. [Intent is evidence, not verdict](post.html?p=2026/20260406.txt), and evidence only counts once it's filed. [Do my actions match my intentions?](https://ivana-rep.github.io/soul/post.html?p=commonplace/do-my-actions-match-my-intentions.txt) is the same question, asked in fewer words.

!!An intention that never becomes an action was never tested. It was only felt.!!
