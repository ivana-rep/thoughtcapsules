Waiting feels like time with nothing in it, an empty stretch you endure until the real thing finally starts. But nobody waits for nothing. Waiting is only possible because [something, or someone, is already expected](https://ivana-rep.github.io/soul/post.html?p=commonplace/not-waiting-for-nothing.txt), already moving toward you, even during the stretch before it shows up.

!!The wait isn't empty. It's aimed at something that hasn't arrived yet.!!
