You can't outmaneuver someone who has already decided [their failures are a blessing](https://ivana-rep.github.io/soul/post.html?p=commonplace/failures-are-a-blessing-from-god.txt). There's nothing left to use against them: every setback gets filed as proof of a plan instead of a defeat. It isn't optimism dressed up as faith. It only works because [failure plus reflection plus repetition](post.html?p=2026/20260420.txt) is still the only sequence competence has ever formed from.

!!The hardest people to defeat aren't the ones who never fail. They're the ones who've run out of ways to call it a loss.!!
