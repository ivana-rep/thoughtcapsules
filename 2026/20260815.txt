Willpower rarely survives contact with an unchanged environment. The same room, the same hour, the same trigger produces the same relapse it produced last time, no matter how much resolve you bring into it. [Willpower was never the fix worth reaching for first](post.html?p=2026/20260516.txt): building a room where the disciplined choice is also the easiest one does more than any amount of resolve.

!!Discipline is what you need when the environment hasn't been built yet.!!
