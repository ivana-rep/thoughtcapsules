Work you do because you have to earns you today: the deadline closed, the obligation met, the paycheck that follows. Work you choose to do beyond that is what earns tomorrow, the skill nobody assigned you, the hour spent on something with no immediate claim on it. [Giving without waiting for it back](post.html?p=2026/20260620.txt) runs on the same logic: the return isn't the point, so its absence never bankrupts you.

!!The work no one required of you is the only kind that compounds.!!
