[The story](https://ivana-rep.github.io/soul/post.html?p=bible/matthew_14-13-21.txt) is simple: a crowd, no food, five loaves and two fish among thousands of people. The disciples reason in scarcity. Count what's available, and when the math doesn't work, send everyone away. Five loaves and two fish treated as an answer instead of a starting point.

The instruction back is simpler than an explanation: bring them here. Abundance isn't attracted from outside. It's already inside what little you have, waiting to be released. The difference was never the resources. It was how they were being looked at.

[Two ways to watch](post.html?p=2026/20260705.txt) someone else's win run the same math: the identical event reads as verdict or as evidence, depending on which frame you bring to it first.

!!Everyone ate until they were satisfied, and there was surplus left over. Not scraps. What changed wasn't the supply. It was whether it was being counted or used.!!
