Monotony contracts perceived time. Novelty dilates it. Days that repeat without variation compress into a blur in memory, while new experience does two things at once: it's remembered in more detail, and it makes the stretch of time containing it feel longer while it's happening.

This isn't just an effect of memory looking backward. It changes what a year actually contains. A life with more new things in it isn't just more memorable, it's subjectively longer, because density of experience is what time is made of, not the number on a calendar.

The counterintuitive part: chasing comfort by minimizing disruption is also chasing a shorter life, in the only sense that's actually felt from the inside.

!!Comfort keeps things easy. It also keeps things short.!!
