[One command](https://ivana-rep.github.io/soul/post.html?p=bible/luke_17-32.txt): don't look back. She looked, and turned into a pillar of salt. Frozen on the spot, never entering what she was walking toward.

The point isn't really the specific danger she was fleeing. It's that you can't move forward while still glancing at what you were freed from. Not the leaving itself. The looking back at it is what stalls a life.

[Reconstruction](post.html?p=2026/20260401.txt) made a related but different point: nostalgia edits the past into evidence, using memory as an argument. This is the more literal version. Some kinds of looking back don't distort the past. They just refuse to let you leave it.

!!Her heart never fully left the place she was walking away from. That's the whole warning: leaving isn't only walking forward, it's not turning around.!!
