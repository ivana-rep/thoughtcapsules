2026_08_23 | Not earned, just given
↳ [back to full archive](2026/2026_full-archive.html)
↳ [back to index](index.html)

A lot of your relationship with the people closest to you gets projected onto everything else. If you've spent enough time feeling like a burden, too much, not enough, it's easy to start approaching everyone that way: apologizing for taking up space, hiding the parts that might not pass inspection, treating belonging as something that fluctuates with your latest performance.

[A child running into her parent's room at 2am](https://ivana-rep.github.io/soul/post.html?p=bible/ephesians_3-12.txt) doesn't stop to ask whether she's been good enough to deserve entry. She just goes, because she already knows where she belongs.

That's the difference worth noticing: one version of belonging has to be maintained. The other one just has to be remembered.

!!You don't get to belong somewhere by never needing anything from it. Belonging was never the reward for being easy.!!
