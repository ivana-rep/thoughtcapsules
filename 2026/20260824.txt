Before choosing, name three ways it goes wrong. Not to talk yourself out of it. To find out if you've actually looked.

If you can't come up with three failure modes, you haven't examined the decision. You've just agreed with your own preference and called it thinking.

[The five percent](post.html?p=2026/20260509.txt) argued for spending your attention only on the decisions that matter. This is what spending it looks like: not more time deliberating in general, but a specific move, reversing the plan and asking what breaks it before you commit to it.

!!Optimism by default isn't confidence. It's a decision you never actually made.!!
