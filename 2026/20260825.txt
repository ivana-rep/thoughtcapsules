Time can be converted into money. Sit in the right room long enough, learn the right skill, put in the right hours, and money follows. The exchange rate isn't fixed, but it exists.

Money can't buy you more time. It can buy back some of what you already spent: comfort, convenience, someone else doing the tedious part. But the total supply doesn't move. Every hour spent earning is an hour already gone from the count, and no amount earned brings it back.

!!You can always make more money. You can never make more time. Spend accordingly.!!
