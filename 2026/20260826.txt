A writer opened a channel to receive manuscripts from readers, with two rules: no first drafts, and nothing generated by a machine. Not because a good sentence needs a human hand to be good. Because the point was never the sentence. It was the person who produced it, and what producing it did to them.

[Writing to inhabit yourself](post.html?p=2026/20260613.txt) isn't the same as writing to be read. But the people worth reading are usually doing both at once: writing because they can't not, hoping someone else who also can't not will notice.

!!He didn't want authors chasing visibility. He wanted readers who write.!!
