Nietzsche's five examples of the Übermensch include princes, generals, a messiah. Titles that sound like domination. But the crown was never the point. What made each of them extraordinary was a disposition that would have shown up with or without the throne.

[Character as choice](post.html?p=2026/20260730.txt) isn't built by the position you land in. It's what decides what you do with whatever position you get. Strip the title away and the person underneath is either still remarkable or was never the source of it.

!!The rank was incidental. The character wasn't.!!
