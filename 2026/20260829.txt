Phil Jackson used to say building a team was a spiritual practice. That sounds abstract until you notice what it actually asks of you: stop tracking what you personally get out of it.

A customer measures value received against price paid. Someone who belongs to something doesn't run that calculation, not out of generosity, but because belonging and accounting are two different modes entirely. The moment you start keeping score of your own return, you've already stepped outside the thing you were trying to join.

[The fan isn't a customer](post.html?p=2026/20260722.txt) for the same reason: what makes a club worth loving is exactly the part that can't be extracted or exchanged.

!!You can be part of something bigger than yourself, or you can keep your own stake separate. Not both at once.!!
