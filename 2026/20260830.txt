Watching the score doesn't change the score. The only lever you actually hold is input: next rep, next decision, next hour spent well or badly. Everything downstream follows that, whether you watch it happen or not.

What you do for people compounds quietly over years. What you say about yourself is usually forgotten by the following week.

[What earns the future](post.html?p=2026/20260816.txt) is the same asymmetry from the other side: unrequired action outlasts self-description every time.

!!Nobody remembers what you said about yourself. Some people never forget what you did for them.!!
