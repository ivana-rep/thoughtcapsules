Philosopher Michael Huemer runs through the classic skeptical arguments — infinite regress, unreliable methods, no way outside your own mind to check it against the world — and treats [none of them as something to accept](https://fakenous.substack.com/p/the-lure-of-radical-skepticism). Their job is different: expose a fault line in how you think you know things, without offering skepticism itself as the fix.

[The regress stops somewhere, or it doesn't](post.html?p=2026/20260301.txt). [The gap between appearance and the thing itself](post.html?p=2026/20260324.txt) looked fatal until it turned out perspective isn't interposition. Both arguments earn their keep by breaking, not by winning.

!!A good skeptical argument was never meant to be believed. It was meant to show you exactly where your confidence gets thin.!!
