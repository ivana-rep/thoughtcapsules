2026_09_02 | The cheaper option

↳ [back to index](index.html)

A state has two ways to answer growing inequality between capital and labor: redistribute, or repress. Economists Daron Acemoglu, Arda Gitmez, and Mehdi Shadmehr [model the choice formally](https://www.nber.org/papers/w35336) and find it isn't ideological. As automation raises capital's share of income and the threat of revolt grows with it, repression becomes systematically the cheaper option, not the crueler one.

Democracies hold only as long as redistribution stays less expensive than control, and that threshold moves with how concentrated the technology gets. Like [an electorate that stops following complex arguments](post.html?p=2026/20260312.txt), nobody has to choose the outcome on purpose.

!!It only has to be cheaper than the alternative, and stay that way long enough.!!
