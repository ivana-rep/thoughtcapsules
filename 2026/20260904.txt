Excellence is inversely proportional to the number of things you're committed to. Depth requires exclusion, not more input. The clearest marker of it: a master can explain the same idea at any resolution, a sentence, a paragraph, a chapter, and stay coherent across all three. The novice can only repeat it at the resolution they memorized.

[What looks like one virtue is usually several](post.html?p=2026/20260808.txt), doing their work quietly enough to pass for one. Same principle from the other side: what looks like many separate facts is usually one idea, compressed or expanded to fit the room it's given.

!!If you can only say it one way, you don't understand it. You've memorized it.!!
