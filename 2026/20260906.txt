Jung's archetypes (Shadow, Child, Father, Anima) work as instruments: they give you a pattern to recognize in yourself before you have language for it. [Deleuze's objection cuts at the model underneath them](https://miniphilosophy.substack.com/p/the-father-the-extrovert-and-the). A central essence with derived branches, the shape every archetype theory assumes, is a cultural habit, not a fact about minds. His alternative is the rhizome: grass, fungus, something that spreads laterally with no center and no hierarchy.

Humans classify compulsively and [no classification exhausts what it's sorting](post.html?p=2026/20260805.txt). An archetype can stay a heuristic, a rough pattern to check yourself against, without hardening into a definition of what you are.

!!A tool for recognizing yourself and a cage built to contain you can look identical from outside. The difference is whether you can still walk out of it.!!
