In a 1960 letter, Hermann Hesse tells a correspondent that authentic work, done with real inner conviction, works as a magnet no matter how alone it feels to produce it: [it draws unknown friends](https://jamesclear.com/3-2-1/august-27-2026) who go looking for you, not the other way around.

That's the inversion worth sitting with. Chase an audience and you get visibility. Do the work for what it actually is, and the people who matter find it on their own; [someone building the same kind of channel](post.html?p=2026/20260826.txt) wanted exactly that, not readers, but people who also can't not write.

!!You don't attract the right people by aiming at them. You attract them by not aiming at them at all.!!
