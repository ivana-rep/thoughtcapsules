[Emilio Dal Bo looks back over his own life](https://open.substack.com/pub/emiliodalbo/p/lettera-di-ringraziamento) and finds a teacher in every obstacle: every rejection, every failure, every absence functioned as sandpaper on raw wood, whether or not anyone intended it to. The real debt of gratitude isn't owed to declared benefactors. It's owed to whoever held the other end of the friction without meaning to teach you anything.

[Wisdom comes from friction, not enlightenment](post.html?p=2026/20260201.txt), and this is the same claim seen from the inside: the shaping doesn't require a teacher who knows they're teaching.

!!Nobody has to mean to sand you down for the sanding to work.!!
