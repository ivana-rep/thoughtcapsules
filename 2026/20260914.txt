A routine that keeps growing more elaborate looks obsessive from the outside. Maybe it is. But most of my negative triggers trace back to [boredom](post.html?p=2026/20260113.txt), not to the routine itself — the complexity isn't decoration, it's ballast against the thing that actually derails days. [Obsession can be fuel or self-sabotage](post.html?p=2026/20260124.txt); the difference was never the intensity, it's what the intensity is standing in for.

!!A routine built to outrun boredom isn't the same disorder as one built to outrun yourself.!!
