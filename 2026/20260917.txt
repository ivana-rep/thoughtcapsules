You feel pressure exactly when your decisions count and other people depend on the outcome. That's not a threat signal, it's a relevance signal. [Without pressure you don't find out what you're made of](post.html?p=2026/20260614.txt) — but the deeper point cuts the other way: the absence of pressure isn't calm, it's irrelevance. Most of what passes for ambition is unresolved pain looking for a project to attach to; the people who get somewhere are the ones practicing the parts nobody wants to practice, pressure included.

!!Feeling pressure means you matter enough for your decision to have consequences. Feeling nothing means you don't, yet.!!
