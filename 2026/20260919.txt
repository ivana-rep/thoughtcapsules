The senses interpret, they don't record. What you call reality is already a construction by the time it reaches you — which is why certainty about what's "really" out there is unavailable in principle, not just in practice. Nietzsche: there are no facts, only interpretations. Einstein: reality is a persistent illusion. [Evolution doesn't filter for truth either](post.html?p=2026/20260731.txt) — it filters for what works, and a working fiction beats a broken one every time.

None of this is an argument for despair. Knowing the ground moves doesn't mean there's no ground to stand on. It means the standing was always more active than it felt.

!!If reality was always a construction, the question was never "what's really there" but "what are you building it out of."!!
