Reading biography is how you borrow pattern recognition for problems you haven't hit yet. Someone already solved yours, in a different century, under a different job title — the solution is sitting in the archive, waiting to be recognized rather than invented from scratch. [Fluency is pattern recognition; understanding is knowing why the pattern holds](post.html?p=2026/20260403.txt). Biography hands you the pattern first. The understanding, if it comes, comes later, usually only after you needed it.

!!You don't have to live a problem to recognize its shape. You just need someone who did, and wrote it down honestly.!!
