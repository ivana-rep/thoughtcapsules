2026_10_03 | The lost excuse
↳ [back to full archive](2026/2026_full-archive.html)
↳ [back to index](index.html)

"I couldn't figure it out" used to be an acceptable ending. The boiler, the form, the settings buried three menus deep: sometimes you gave up, called someone, or lived with it. [Vittorio Ray notices that this option is disappearing](https://iltuffatore.substack.com/p/tuffi173). With a machine that answers any practical question in seconds, not managing stops being a limit and becomes a choice. He also notes people turning to AI for reassurance and company, not only for answers.

The first point holds. Once the answer is a question away, not acting is no longer ignorance. It's a decision, and honesty means naming it as one. But I wouldn't call it negligence. Growth for me means reading imperfect days as data, not as verdicts, and that includes the days when I didn't figure it out.

The second point is the one that matters. Asking a machine for comfort is one more way of filling a silence that feels threatening, and I know that move well. The emptiness isn't dangerous, and the hunger underneath it has a different address: [come to Me, all you who are weary, and I will give you rest](https://ivana-rep.github.io/soul/post.html?p=bible/matthew_11-28-30.txt).

!!A machine can answer every question except the one the silence was asking.!!
