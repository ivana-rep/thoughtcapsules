The center of science has moved before: Italy around 1600, England and France around 1700, Germany around 1900, the United States from the 1960s. [Adam Frank argues](https://bigthinkmedia.substack.com/p/who-pays-the-cost-when-americans) it follows whoever stays committed to it, and that rejecting science is rarely an intellectual position. It is tribal: you refuse a finding because of who holds it. The bill arrives slowly, and it is paid by everyone, not only by the people who refused.

The mechanism goes well beyond science. [A belief already contains a direction](post.html?p=2026/20260316.txt): when you adopt a belief to belong, you also adopt where it takes you.

Frank counts the cost in lost competitiveness. I would count it earlier. Refusing what you can see in order to stay with your side is a form of dishonesty, first of all toward yourself, and it would be wrong even if it cost nothing. Integrity means belief follows the evidence, not the group.

!!Believing in order to belong is a lie you tell yourself first. Everyone else pays for it later.!!
