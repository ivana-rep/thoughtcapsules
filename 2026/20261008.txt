Repeating the same success feels flat. [James Clear's explanation](https://jamesclear.com/3-2-1/september-10-2026): "Winning isn't what makes you feel good. Growth makes you feel good." His remedy for feeling stuck is to learn something new, and instead of dreaming about the ideal version, to try the real thing for five minutes, at a smaller scale.

The method is right. [A first step only has to exist](post.html?p=2026/20260624.txt), and five minutes is small enough to exist today.

The reason is weaker. If you grow because growth feels good, you stop the day it doesn't, and most real change happens on days that feel like nothing at all. Growth through consistency has to survive the flat weeks, which means it can't depend on the feeling. It depends on doing the small thing again because it is right, not because it rewards you.

!!Growth that runs on feeling good lasts until the first flat week.!!
