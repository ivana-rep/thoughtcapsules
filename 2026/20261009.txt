"If you want to live a happy life, tie it to a goal, not to people or things." [Thomas Oppong attributes it to Einstein](https://postanly.substack.com/p/einsteins-happiness-rule-on-the-good) and builds on it: things lose their shine because the mind gets used to what it owns, people have needs of their own and can't hold you up, a goal gives you direction.

The first half is sound. [Life is not measured by how much you own](https://ivana-rep.github.io/soul/post.html?p=bible/luke_12-15.txt), and asking another person to make you complete puts on them a weight nobody can carry.

The second half replaces one fragile anchor with another. A goal can fail. It can be reached and leave you empty. It can quietly become your identity, so that falling short of it feels like falling short as a person. The Christian answer starts somewhere else: [you were loved before you achieved anything](https://ivana-rep.github.io/soul/post.html?p=bible/romans_5-8.txt). A goal can give direction. It can't give a foundation.

!!Whatever you tie your life to gets tested the day you fail it.!!
