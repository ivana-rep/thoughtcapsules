2026_10_10 | Thoroughly bored
↳ [back to full archive](2026/2026_full-archive.html)
↳ [back to index](index.html)

Make something with your hands. Read with a pencil. Learn a poem by heart. Eat without phones on the table. Write a letter and post it. Let yourself get thoroughly bored. [Emilio Dal Bo lists about seventy of these](https://www.trasumanare.it/p/restare-umani-ai), deliberately small, as a way to stay attached to a carnal, imperfect human life while everything around turns artificial.

The practices are good. [Boredom especially](post.html?p=2026/20260113.txt): it is the one most people now never reach.

The framing is narrower than it needs to be. Dal Bo presents these acts as resistance to the machine. But people filled every silence long before screens existed. What boredom exposes is not a lack of stimulation; it is a restlessness that was there before it. Augustine named it sixteen centuries ago: [our heart is restless until it rests in You](https://ivana-rep.github.io/soul/post.html?p=saints/augustine-of-hippo.txt). Silence matters not as a protest, but because it is where that restlessness can finally be heard.

!!Silence isn't a protest against technology. It's where you find out what you were filling.!!
