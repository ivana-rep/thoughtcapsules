2026_10_14 | A poor success
↳ [back to full archive](2026/2026_full-archive.html)
↳ [back to index](index.html)

John D. Rockefeller, the richest man of his age, [quoted by Shane Parrish](https://fs.blog/brain-food/april-26-2026/): "Wealth isn't a distinction. If I had no other achievement to my credit than the accumulation of wealth, then I had made a poor success of life."

He is right, and coming from him it carries weight. But the sentence keeps the same frame it criticizes: life as a list of achievements, with wealth demoted to a minor entry. Swap the money for good works and the ledger is still a ledger.

Jesus went further. [Life is not measured by how much you own](https://ivana-rep.github.io/soul/post.html?p=bible/luke_12-15.txt), he said, and then told the [parable of the rich fool](https://ivana-rep.github.io/soul/post.html?p=bible/luke_12-13-21.txt#v13): a man who plans bigger barns and dies that night. The man's failure isn't a short list of achievements. It's that he trusted what he had stored up. The question isn't how much you've accumulated, of anything. It's what you're relying on.

!!Wealth isn't a distinction. Neither is the list that replaces it.!!
