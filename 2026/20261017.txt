[James Clear](https://jamesclear.com/3-2-1/june-25-2026) asks: "Where is your energy better spent right now: pushing harder or planting yourself in better ground?"

The question is right. The default answer is pushing harder, because effort is the part you can feel, while the same effort gives different results depending on where it's spent.

But Clear's ground is circumstance: a better job, better people, a better environment. All real, and all able to change overnight. Jeremiah used the same image and went deeper: those who trust in the Lord [are like trees planted along a riverbank](https://ivana-rep.github.io/soul/post.html?p=bible/jeremiah_17-7-10.txt), "not bothered by the heat or worried by long months of drought." The ground that matters is what you trust. It's the only kind that still holds when the circumstances fail, and the tree doesn't produce fruit by straining.

!!Before pushing harder, check what you're rooted in.!!
