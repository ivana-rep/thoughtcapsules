Doris Lessing's advice on reading, [shared by Shane Parrish](https://fs.blog/brain-food/september-14-2025/): browse, choose what appeals to you, drop what bores you, and never read anything "because you feel you ought." A book that bores you at twenty may open doors at fifty, and the reverse. "Don't read a book out of its right time for you."

For most books she's right. Forcing one at the wrong moment teaches you to dislike it, and you lose it for the moment it would have mattered.

For one book she isn't. Scripture isn't read for appetite, because appetite isn't the point. Read daily out of commitment, on the dry days as much as the days it speaks, it works on a different logic. [There is a time for everything](https://ivana-rep.github.io/soul/post.html?p=bible/ecclesiastes_3-1-8.txt), and you don't know in advance which morning is the right one. Showing up is how you're there when it comes.

!!Some books wait for you. Some you wait for.!!
