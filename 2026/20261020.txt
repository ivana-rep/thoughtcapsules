2026_10_20 | No need to sparkle
↳ [back to full archive](2026/2026_full-archive.html)
↳ [back to index](index.html)

"You have to run your own race," [James Clear writes](https://jamesclear.com/3-2-1/november-13-2025). "Problems begin the moment you start comparing your results to someone who is playing under different conditions." His remedy: keep the comparison internal. Are you getting a little better today? He closes with Virginia Woolf: "No need to hurry. No need to sparkle. No need to be anybody but oneself."

The first half is right. Comparing yourself to others measures your inside against their outside.

The remedy keeps the scoreboard and moves it indoors. [Comparing with your past self](post.html?p=2026/20260607.txt) is healthier, but your worth still depends on progress, and a bad month becomes a verdict. Woolf's line goes further than Clear's advice, and the reason it can be true is older than both. To sparkle is to perform for people who judge by what they see. God doesn't judge that way: [people judge by outward appearance, but the Lord looks at the heart](https://ivana-rep.github.io/soul/post.html?p=bible/1-samuel_16-7.txt). When the judgment that matters most looks past the performance, the performance loses its point. Paul draws the practical consequence: [pay careful attention to your own work, and you won't need to compare yourself to anyone else](https://ivana-rep.github.io/soul/post.html?p=bible/galatians_6-4.txt). The work stays. The audience goes.

!!You don't need to sparkle for someone who looks at the heart.!!
