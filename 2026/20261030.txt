[Saffana](https://marketingrealitypod.substack.com/p/risk-vs-rizq-a-paradigm-shifting), who writes a newsletter on marketing and mindset, sets two words side by side. Rizq is Arabic for what is meant for you, your provision, from one of the names of God in Islam, Al-Razzaq, the Provider. Risk, she says, was born when European traders misunderstood the word (an etymology that is disputed). Her proposal: stop thinking of yourself as an owner and think of yourself as a "temporary custodian" of what you have. "You're not responsible for outcome, just effort. Come what may, always meant for you."

The first half holds, and Christians will recognize it. [Everything in the heavens and on earth is Yours, O Lord](https://ivana-rep.github.io/soul/post.html?p=bible/1-chronicles_29-11.txt): what you hold is on loan, and what's asked of you is faithfulness with it. The results are out of your hands, and that frees you.

The second half goes wrong. "Whatever comes was meant for you" turns everything that happens into a fair allocation. Some things that happen are simply wrong: a betrayal, a theft, an injustice. Calling them destiny lets the person who did them off the hook. Joseph, sold into slavery by his brothers, doesn't tell them it was meant. He says: [you intended to harm me, but God intended it all for good](https://ivana-rep.github.io/soul/post.html?p=bible/genesis_50-20.txt). The harm stays harm, and God still works through it.

!!A custodian answers to a Person, not to fate.!!
